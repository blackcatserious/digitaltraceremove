When negative content rises quickly, the first 24 hours determine the outcome. This briefing outlines a repeatable playbook:

1. **Secure intake & NDA.** Establish a private channel, gather evidence, and confirm escalation paths.
2. **Diagnosis & plan.** Map the search results, source platforms, and legal options.
3. **Execution & reporting.** Launch removals, track changes, and deliver evidence logs to stakeholders.

Use this framework when responding to sudden press, doxxing, or coordinated smear activity.
