---

Takedowns fail most often when documentation is incomplete. Evidence logs ensure every escalation includes:

- Timestamped screenshots and URLs.
- Source platform policies violated.
- Stakeholder approvals and chain of custody.

When combined with a secure communication channel, evidence logs speed approval cycles and keep internal teams aligned.
